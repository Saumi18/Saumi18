### 🐺 The Story So Far

I'm an undergraduate at **IIT Jodhpur** studying Electrical Engineering. Every house needs words. Mine would read something like: understand a system fully before you touch it, then build the thing properly the first time.

I like interdisciplinary problems that blend theory with engineering: building multi-agent systems that reason over real codebases, modeling credit and market risk, decoding RF signals, or pushing pixels through an FPGA pipeline at 120 FPS. Right now I'm deep in **LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation, and multi-agent orchestration**, while keeping one foot in **quant finance** and **hardware and signal processing**.

Open to **remote internships and research collaborations** in Applied AI, ML Engineering, or Quant Research. A raven reaches me fastest at the email below.
