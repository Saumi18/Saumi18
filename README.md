### 🐺 About Me

I'm an undergraduate at **IIT Jodhpur** studying Electrical Engineering. Every good engineer needs a sigil, mine's the wolf: I hunt problems in packs of first principles, and I don't stop until the system actually works end-to-end.

I like interdisciplinary problems that blend theory with engineering: building agentic RAG systems, modeling credit and market risk, decoding RF signals, or pushing pixels through an FPGA pipeline at 120 FPS. Lately I've been deep in **LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation, and multi-agent systems**, while keeping one foot in **quant finance** and **hardware/signal processing**.

Currently raising ravens for **remote internships & research collaborations** in Applied AI / ML Engineering / Quant Research. 🐦‍⬛
